HOW IT WORKS
One connection, lots of little moments.

Once connected, open LED Matrix for drawings and messages, RGB Light for colors and animations, or Our Kitty to care for your shared virtual pet.

Your key stays in your browser. Your Active Key is used only for the Adafruit IO connection; it is not built into this public website. Each person signs in with their own account and key.
No Arduino upload is needed to switch between these features. Your existing sketch handles the LED features. The shared kitty lives in its own Adafruit IO feed, so no Arduino upload is needed.
